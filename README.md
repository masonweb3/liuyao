# Liuyao · 六爻

**[sixyao.app](https://sixyao.app)** · [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

A six-line (六爻, *liùyáo*) I Ching divination site in a quiet Chinese style. Write down your question, settle your mind, toss three coins six times, and read the hexagram. The interface is in Simplified Chinese; the 64 hexagram pages also come in Traditional Chinese (Taiwan usage) under `/zh-hant/gua/`. There is also a Chinese almanac (黄历) at `/huangli/`: each day's recommended and avoided activities, clashes and day officers, computed with tyme4ts, plus the 24 solar terms with their pentads and seasonal notes, in both scripts. Within it, `/zeri/` lists each month's suitable days for six occasions (moving house, weddings, opening a business, travel, renovation, engagements), with an optional zodiac filter that stays in the browser. A BaZi (八字, Four Pillars) chart page at `/bazi/` computes the pillars, ten gods, hidden stems, na yin, symbolic stars, branch relations, luck pillars and annual pillars in the browser from a birth date (with an optional birth city, so overseas births use the local time zone including historical daylight saving). It lays out the chart only, with no readings, followed by a plain-language walkthrough of what the chart contains (the day master, the five elements and ten gods, the luck pillars); the birth data is never uploaded and is saved only on the device if you choose to. A Plum Blossom (梅花易数, *Meihua Yishu*) page at `/meihua/` casts a hexagram from the moment (lunar year, month, day and double hour, in Beijing time) or from one or two numbers you name, and reads it by the body and use trigrams; it shares the question check, the safety screens and the one-question-one-cast history with the coin casting (Simplified Chinese only for now).

- **The chart is computed, not generated.** Najia (纳甲), the six relations, world and response lines, six spirits, void branches, hidden spirits and strength are all worked out by deterministic code. The same cast always gives the same chart. Tests cover the worked examples from *Zengshan Buyi* (增删卜易) and 4,096 parity fixtures.
- **Good or bad is decided by rules**, with the reasons shown, never by a language model.
- **[TypeSafe Jev](https://docs.typesafe.ai) only reads the question**: it sorts it into a topic, which picks the use god (用神), and screens out self-harm, emergencies and gambling. If Jev is unavailable, you pick the topic yourself and everything still works.
- **Readings** pair the original judgments and line texts (public domain) with plain-language commentary written for this project.
- **On a phone** you can hold the 摇 button, or turn on shake mode and shake the phone itself.
- **Free, no ads, no sign-in.** Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track individuals; there are no other trackers. Past casts, and what you later note about how things turned out, stay in your browser; the optional reminder goes into your own calendar and carries only the date and the hexagram: a calendar file generated on your device (on iPhone and iPad, generated by the server from the link and not stored), or a prefilled Google Calendar event; no email, no push notifications. The question is sent once to Jev for classification and is not logged.

## Stack

Astro 7 on Cloudflare Workers, TypeScript, [tyme4ts](https://github.com/6tail/tyme4ts) for the calendar, Vitest. Every page is prerendered; the only server routes are `/api/judge`, which calls Jev, and `/api/remind.ics`, which builds the reminder calendar file for iPhone and iPad.
